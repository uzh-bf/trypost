# DF editorial recipes

Use these recipes in an Idea's writing assistant with the DF style. Each recipe is a starting prompt, not a caption template or a factual source. Replace the bracketed fields and paste the complete source material into the Generate prompt. Generate does not send the existing Idea body. New text follows your TryPost language; rewrites retain the source language.

Review the result, insert it into the Idea, then save and reopen the complete Idea. Select only the intended caption and choose **Use selection** to open the post composer. Media and labels follow the existing handoff. Without a non-empty selection, **Create post** transfers the complete body, or the title when the body is empty. Handoff does not save unsaved Idea edits.

A carousel brief describes the slides; it does not render artwork. A Reel brief describes the production; it does not produce video or subtitles. Keep original portraits, footage and design work with the normal human production workflow. Generation uses the selected model and its normal provider billing; these recipes do not submit anything automatically.

## 1. Student opportunity or event

```text
Prepare a DF editorial draft for the following student opportunity or event.
Audience: [who this is for and what they already know]
Format: [Instagram carousel or Reel]
Primary action: [one supplied action and its exact destination]
Source material: [complete verified facts, dates, deadline, location or format, eligibility, cost, exact links, and image or footage information]

Use a direct, approachable opening that makes the practical relevance clear from the supplied facts. Keep one primary call to action. Preserve Swiss German spelling when writing German. Use emoji and hashtags only where they help this audience.

Return separate sections:
CAPTION: the proposed publishable caption, with no production instructions.
PRODUCTION NOTES: for a carousel, give each slide one purpose, concise proposed text and an asset need. For a Reel, separate spoken text, shot suggestions, cover text and subtitle text.
SOURCE CHECKS: list missing or uncertain facts and checks needed before publication. Keep them out of the caption.

Use only the supplied facts. Leave missing details for review. Preserve exact names, dates, URLs and quotations. Suggest no speakers, outcomes or testimonials that the source does not supply.
```

## 2. People and research

```text
Prepare a DF editorial draft introducing a person, research topic or alumni experience.
Audience: [students, researchers, practitioners, or another supplied audience]
Format: [Instagram carousel or Reel]
Primary action: [one supplied action or question]
Source material: [approved biography or interview answers, exact quotes, research summary, source links, and available portraits or footage]

Lead with a concrete question, experience or research issue relevant to this audience. Let the supplied person's own account carry the story. Use the shared DF style without forcing informal language into an academic introduction.

Return separate sections:
CAPTION: the proposed publishable caption.
PRODUCTION NOTES: for a carousel, give a cover and one purpose per slide, with concise copy and an asset need. For a Reel, separate spoken text, shot suggestions, cover text and subtitle text. Keep long source passages in the brief and propose shorter slide text for review.
SOURCE CHECKS: identify missing facts, attribution, image permissions and review needs. Mark proposed paraphrases explicitly. Keep exact quotations unchanged.

Treat examples as guidance for structure and tone only. Derive all names, roles, affiliations, research findings and dates from the supplied source. Make no unsupported claims about impact, success or audience reactions.
```

## 3. Website news for manual Magnolia transfer

```text
Prepare a DF website news draft for human review and manual transfer into Magnolia.
Audience: [intended website readers]
Purpose: [what readers should learn or do]
Length: [approximate word count]
Source material: [complete verified facts, exact dates and links, approved quotations, source references, and image description and credit]

Return separate sections:
TITLE: a clear, factual headline.
LEAD: the central news and why it matters to the supplied audience, using supported facts.
BODY: connected paragraphs with the necessary context and any supplied action or link.
IMAGE CAPTION AND CREDIT: use only the supplied image information.
SOURCE CHECKS: references, missing details, attribution and approvals needed before transfer. Keep internal notes outside the article text.

Use the shared DF style. Preserve the source's level of certainty. Keep quotations exact and identify proposed paraphrases. Describe relevance without inventing significance, achievements, results or endorsements. The result is a draft; do not claim that it has been published or transferred to the CMS.
```
